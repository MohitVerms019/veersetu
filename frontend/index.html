Platform Benefits

Why Choose VEER SETU?

From education grants to healthcare coverage, VEER SETU aggregates every verified welfare scheme under one secure roof, purpose-built for India's military families.

Education & Scholarships

Access the Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS) offering up to ₹3,000/month for wards of serving and retired personnel. Navigate KSB education portals, Army Welfare Education Society (AWES) school admissions, and state-level military scholarship programmes — all in one place.

Learn More (Login required)

Healthcare & ECHS

Understand your entitlements under the Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme (ECHS). Get guidance on the 64KB smart card for cashless treatment at over 400 polyclinics and empanelled hospitals across India. Access Army Group Insurance Fund medical benefits and claim procedures.

Learn More (Login required)

Financial & Pension

Navigate the SPARSH pension portal for seamless pension disbursement. Understand AGIF life coverage of up to ₹1 Crore, disability pensions, liberalised pension policies, and the Army Central Welfare Fund. All schemes verified against official Ministry of Defence circulars.

Learn More (Login required)

Family & Veer Nari

Dedicated pathways for Veer Naris — war widows and next-of-kin of fallen soldiers. Access the Directorate of Indian Army Veterans (DIAV), Zila Sainik Board counselling, Army Wives Welfare Association programmes, and priority housing allotment schemes for bereaved families.

Learn More (Login required)
