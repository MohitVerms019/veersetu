Terms and Conditions

1. Academic Demonstration

Veer Setu is an academic prototype demonstrating a welfare platform architecture. It does not represent an official Ministry of Defence portal.

2. Data Privacy

Your information is processed securely and never shared with third parties. Passwords are hashed using Argon2id. Personal data complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

3. Eligibility Verification

Army identity verification is conducted by authorized administrators only. Fake credentials are grounds for account termination.

4. Welfare Information

Scheme information links directly to authentic government portals (ksb.gov.in, echs.gov.in, sparsh.defencepension.gov.in). We do not mediate applications.

5. Security

Accounts are protected with rate-limiting, session management, and multi-factor authentication support.
