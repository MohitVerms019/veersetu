Platform Ethos & Genesis

Honoring Valor, Empowering Families.

VEER SETU was founded on a singular patriotic premise: No Indian soldier, veteran, or martyr family should struggle through bureaucratic ambiguity to receive the welfare benefits they have rightfully earned through selfless service.

Our Vision

To guarantee complete, dignified, and frictionless digital access to all welfare, educational scholarships, medical coverage, and pension entitlements for every serving soldier, veteran, and martyr family across Bharat.

Our Mission

To bridge the informational divide between defense agencies (Kendriya Sainik Board, PCDA, ECHS, AGIF, AWHO) and military families through an intuitive, accessible, and secure single-window digital sanctuary.

Foundational Principles

The Four Pillars of Veer Setu

Every feature and workflow on this platform is built upon four unshakeable values.

Honor & Dignity

Treating every soldier, veteran, and Veer Nari with unconditional respect and prioritized digital care.

Total Transparency

Explicit eligibility rules, document requirements, and authentic links to official ministry portals.

Universal Access

Accessible on low-bandwidth connections in remote border outposts and rural villages alike.

Veer Nari Care

Dedicated assistance workflows for war widows, battle casualties, and dependent children.

Key Commitments

Strategic Objectives of the Platform

01

Single-Window Clearance

Eliminate the confusion of navigating dozens of disconnected military and government portals.

02

Eligibility Pre-Matching

Match service personnel, veterans, and dependents with schemes tailored to their service category.

03

Document Readiness

Provide step-by-step checklists of discharge books, PPOs, and certificates before application.

04

Digital Welfare Pass

Generate verifiable demonstration passes containing regiment and pre-qualified entitlement tags.

05

Veer Nari Priority

Offer accelerated support desks for war widows, battle casualties, and dependent students.

06

Authentic Redirection

Direct users straight to authoritative government domains (ksb.gov.in, echs.gov.in, sparsh).
